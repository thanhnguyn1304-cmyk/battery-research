ABSTRACT
Antimony@carbon (Sb@C) with high capacity and low reaction potential have been the focus of research on 
sodium storage electrodes. However, it is challenging to obtain the precisely correlation of electrode structure 
parameters on battery capacity in experimental research. Machine learning (ML) is a kind of artificial intelligence 
and can predict the quantitative relationship between material structural and cycle capacity of electrodes by 
building models. Hence, in this work the 6 key structural characteristics parameter of Sb@C electrode are 
investigated for sodium-ion batteries with cycle performance by 4 kinds of algorithms based on ML, and Ada -
boost algorithm embody the best performance with R
2 
values of 0.91 and 0.83 on the test set and training set 
with 10-fold cross-validation, respectively. Furthermore, it is obtained the antimony content (AC) of Sb@C 
composite is the most critical of structural parameters affect the remaining capacity of cycle performance 
through feature screening and SHAP analysis, due to the sodium storage mechanism that the cycle capacity of 
Sb@C electrode are directly affected the high capacity of Sb. Meanwhile, it is accurately predicted the 0.65 – 0.75 
of AC at smaller-size of antimony size (AS) and higher or lower of the intensity ratio for Raman peak (RP) with 
Sb@C electrode is obtained the high cycle capacity with exhaustion method. It corresponds that the stabilized 
structure of the carbon material effectively buffers the volume expansion caused by the addition of appropriate 
AC to reduce the capacity fading. And the higher or lower of RP value improves the electrical conductivity of the 
carbon and facilitates the electron transport. More importantly, the small-sized AS provide more active sites, 
which contribute to the storage and electrochemical reaction activity of sodium ions, thus further enhancing the 
cycle remaining capacity. In addition, the strong generalization ability of built model is validated by other series 
of metal@carbon composites within less 15 % of loss rate. The effective method and new direction with ML- 
screening are obtained to explore the structural parameter of electrode to predict battery cycle capacity for 
providing a comprehensive effective solution.
1. Introduction
Sodium-ion batteries (SIBs) have attracted a lot of attention because 
of low price and abundant sodium resources, which are expected to be 
used in low-speed electric cars, energy storage systems and portable 
electronics. As we all know, it is of great research significance for the 
rational structure of electrode materials with high capacity to improve 
the cycle performance of SIBs [1 – 3] .
Antimony (Sb) has been widely studied as hot spot electrode mate -
rials in SIBs due to the high theoretical capacity of 660 mAh/g and good 
electrical conductivity. However, the larger volume expansion and 
contraction of Sb lead to electrode pulverization and structural collapse 
in the charge-discharge process, thus affecting the cycle performance 
and specific capacity in SIBs [4,5] . Nanosizing Sb can better alleviate the 
stress concentration caused by the volume change to reduce the rupture 
and pulverization of electrode. Alternatively, Sb nanomaterials are 
composited with carbon to form Sb@C, in which the carbon material can 
provide the better electrical conductivity while acting as a buffer against 
volume expansion [6] . Many researchers have carried out different 
structural regulations of Sb@C composites to improve the sodium stor -
age properties. For example, Huang et al., reported 70 wt% Sb in Sb@C 
by solvent method to form composites for SIBs, and the reversible ca -
pacity of 495.2 mAh/g after 100 cycles at a current density of 
125 mAh/g [7] . Subsequently, Nguyen et al. investigated Sb@C

Machine Learning (ML) is the core technology of artificial intelli -
gence research, which is now gradually and widely used in the research 
design of electrode [11] . For example, Liu et al. used ML establish a 
suitable model to efficiently screen the effect of microstructural pa -
rameters of non-graphitic carbon materials based on sodium storage 
mechanism and performance through Web of Science databases [12] . As 
well as Isaiah A. et al. trained the ML models to predict the average 
voltage and volume changes of metal-ion battery electrode materials 
during charging and discharging based on the materials project database
[13] . Subsequently, they successfully developed a tool and proposed 
nearly 5000 candidate electrode materials. Moreover, our group has 
predicted novel photoelectric materials to enhance power conversion 
efficiency by ML [14,15] . All above works show that ML is a highly 
efficient and adaptable method for designing and screening the struc -
tural of materials, providing an important way for the performance 
prediction.
Here, the structure parameters of Sb@C materials have been 
explored with ML to establish between structural parameters and cycle 
performance for SIBs with high remaining capacity in this work. The ML 
workflow for screening the structural parameters of Sb@C electrode 
with high cycle remaining capacity by ML for sodium-ion batteries as 
shown in Fig. 1 . Firstly, 90 data points of original experimental research 
as displayed in Table S5 about the sodium storage of Sb@C composites 
with six key structural feature as inputs and cycle capacity as output 
were collected and extracted from the Web of Science in recent years. 
Then, the model was trained to predict cycle remaining capacity after 
normalization using ML such as Random Forest (RF), eXtreme Gradient 
Boosting(XGBoost), Adaptive Boosting (Adaboost) and Support Vector 
Regression (SVR). The Adaboost model exhibited the highest prediction 
accuracy with analysis by ML performance metrics. Meanwhile, the 
explainable analysis was also carried out by elucidating the effectiveness 
of the model through Shapley values, and the adoption of exhaustive 
method with constructing artificial data of the built model was applied 
to predict relatively precise range of structural characteristics, including 
AC, RP and AS, for the high cycle remaining capacity. Finally, the 
experimental data of sodium storage with other metal@carbon com -
posite, liking Ge@C, Sn@C, Bi@C and Fe@C composites, were used to 
validate the generalization of the model.
2. Results and discussion
ML algorithms including Adaboost, SVR, XGBoost and RF were used 
to construct the training and testing models to establish and evaluate the 
mapping relationship between structural characteristics and cycle per -
formance with high capacity in Sb@C composites,and the structural 
parameters as the input features and the remaining capacity of 100 cy -
cles as the output feature were shown in Table S1 , and the training re -
sults of the models were displayed in Fig. S1 without IC as the input 
features. Although Adaboost had better result compared with other 
models algorithms, the test set R² was 0.57 and the mean square error 
MSE was 54.2, which was relatively low. Because the R² value was used 
to evaluate and quantify the goodness of fit of the ML model and the 
relationship between the independent and dependent variables, and it 
was an important indicator in regression analysis [16] , if the closer the 
R² value is to 1 with the higher the prediction accuracy of cycle 
remaining capacity. To improve the accuracy of the prediction model, 
the initial capacity (IC) was taken into account containing other factors, 
such as electrolyte type, synthesis method and other structure of mate -
rial, etc. to affect remaining capacity of cycle performance and intro -
duced as an input feature using the method of CEP.
The training and testing results of the ML algorithm using 10-fold 
cross-validation after adding IC were shown in Fig. 2 and Table 1 .The 
R² values of the test sets and training sets for AdaBoost, RF, XGboost and 
SVR were 0.83 and 0.91, 0.76 and 0.86, 0.77 and 0.87, 0.77 and 0.87, 
respectively. And the introduction of the initial capacity can contribute 
to improve the predicted performance evaluation metrics of all models 
which were greatly better optimized. At the same time, this also in -
dicates the significant enhancement in the predictive capacity of the 
AdaBoost algorithm models for cycle remaining capacity. What ’ s more, 
the RMSE (43.5) and MAE (37.1) values of the test sets for Adaboost 
models were lower than those of RF (RMSE was 51.8, MAE was 44.1), 
XGboost (RMSE was 50.9, MAE was 43.2) and SVR (RMSE was 44.3, 
MAE was 38.3). This may be because RF, XGBoost, and SVR were prone 
to overfitting on small data sets or have difficulty capturing features 
effectively. The AdaBoost algorithm was simple and computationally 
efficient, and can adapt to data patterns in small datasets more quickly
[17] . In addition, although both AdaBoost and XGBoost were boosting 
algorithms, XGBoost, as a more complex algorithm, usually can fully 
utilize its advantages on large-scale datasets, its complexity and 
computational overhead fail to bring about significant performance 
improvement in small datasets, but instead, it may increase the training 
time and heighten the risk of overfitting [18] . Therefore, the AdaBoost 
algorithm was selected as the best method to establish the quantitative 
relationship between the structural characteristics and the cycle 
remaining capacity on the small datasets. In addition, the 5-fold 
cross-validation of various algorithms were performed to prove the 
robustness of the model shown in Fig. S2 and Table S2 . In contrast, 
10-fold cross-validation of AdaBoost model is slightly better, with both 
RMSE and MAE on the test set lower than 5-fold cross-validation, and 
better generalization ability. And the two folds cross-validation of other 
algorithms also showed the similar results, which is reason that the 
higher percentage of training data could better capture the features,

For analyzing the correlation strength between structural charac -
teristics and cycle remaining capacity of Sb@C composites, the Pearson 
correlation coefficients (r) between each input feature and cycle 
remaining capacity were calculated and shown in Fig. 3 , and it could be 
seen 0.55 of r between AC and RC, which suggested that the AC should 
be considered to optimize the cycle performance with high capacity, and 
Pham et.al [19] reported the antimony content was increased from 0.58 
to 0.64 and the corresponding remaining capacity of cycle performance 
was increased from 498 mAh/g to 548 mAh/g. In addition, there was a 
significant correlation between the RP and AS with RC, and the Pearson 
correlation coefficients were correspond to 0.22 and 0.14, respectively. 
This suggested that the cycle remaining capacity was comparatively 
affected by RP and AS. Moreover, the Pearson correlation coefficient of 
0.72 was obtained and shown a better statistical trend between the 
initial capacity and the output characteristic of cycle remaining capacity 
from the calculation, and it was indicated the cycle remaining capacity 
were greatly affected by the initial capacity. Meanwhile, this was also a 
significant correlation (r = 0.40) between the IC and AC and it was 
consistent with the accordingly raised initial capacity with the increase 
of AC due to the high theoretical capacity of 660 mAh/g for Sb, and 
these are the main reasons why the IC was added as input feature by 
CEP.

3. Conclusion and future
In summary, this work established the prediction model for the high 
remaining capacity of Sb@C electrode for sodium-ion storage based on 
the multiple types of ML algorithm, and it was shown that the Adaboost 
algorithm had the more accurate predictions compared with XGBoost, 
RF, and SVR, and the corresponding R² of the test set reaches 0.83, with 
an average absolute error of 37.1. Moreover, the consistent robustness of 
prediction model was obtained with under different fold cross- 
validation without significant overfitting or underfitting. Meanwhile, 
it quantitatively analyzed the relationship between the structural char -
acteristics of Sb@C composite with the cycle remaining capacity 
through the established prediction model, and the better cycling ca -
pacity could be predicted from the range of Raman peak intensity ratio 
for 0.5 – 1 or 2.5 – 3, antimony size for 3 – 25 nm and antimony content for 
0.65 – 0.75. In addition, the build model of cycle remaining capacity for 
Sb@C composite was verified by the method of data migration with the 
sodium storage properties of other metal@carbon composite, and the 
error rate of the model is less than 15 %. Our work establishes a database 
of structural properties of antimony@carbon (Sb@C) composites and 
analyzes the relationship between structural parameters and sodium 
storage performance using machine learning. An efficient methodology 
is proposed, which helps to correlate the material structure with high 
cycling capacity to design and screen electrode materials for high- 
performance sodium-ion batteries.
